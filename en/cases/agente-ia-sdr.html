AI SDR Agent: over 3,000 conversations and R$ 30k in sales

An artificial intelligence agent I built to work as a sales development rep: it talks to leads, qualifies them, wins back lost customers and books meetings on its own.

I want a project like this
Category
Artificial Intelligence
Industry
Sales & customer service
Deliverable
AI agent
SDR

The goal

Automate the front line of sales with an AI agent working as an SDR: talk to leads, qualify their interest, re-engage customers who had been dropped and hand booked meetings to the sales team — without depending on someone being available at all times.

What the agent does

  • Talks to leads naturally, answering questions about the product or service.
  • Qualifies every contact before taking up the sales team's time.
  • Wins back lost customers by restarting conversations with contacts that had been left aside.
  • Automatically books meetings with people who show real interest.

Results

  • +3,000conversations held
  • +150meetings booked automatically
  • R$ 30kin direct sales generated

Beyond the numbers, the agent started winning back customers who had been dropped, turning previously lost contacts into new sales opportunities.
